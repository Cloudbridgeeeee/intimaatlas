# Product

AI market intelligence software that helps adult wellness retailers understand product assortment, merchandise trends and catalogue gaps using aggregate commercial data.

- Co-founder and CTO: Deborah Adeyemi

- MVP: Catalogue imports and product assortment analytics
- Planned AWS: Bedrock, SageMaker AI, S3, DynamoDB, Lambda
- Business model: Monthly retailer subscriptions
