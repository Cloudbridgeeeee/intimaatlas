How IntimaAtlas onboarding runs

  1. Catalogue and sales import

    Send your product catalogue and weekly sales totals as CSV or from your ecommerce platform.

  2. Category dashboards

    Within a week each category shows its trend, its share of sales and how your range compares.

  3. Monthly assortment review

    Each month buyers receive suggestions to add, keep or reduce lines and log their decision on each.

Questions

What retailers and distributors ask before a demo.

Ask about a demo

Who is IntimaAtlas for?

Adult wellness retailers, distributors and manufacturers. It is a business tool, not a consumer shop.

What data do you need?

Your product catalogue and weekly sales totals per product and store. No shopper names or order details.

What does it cost?

A monthly subscription for each retailer, priced by number of stores. We quote after a demo.

Does it change our catalogue?

No. It reads imports only. Your buyers decide every change to the range.

How often is data refreshed?

Weekly. Sales totals import each Monday and dashboards update the same morning.

Where is our data kept?

In an encrypted AWS store for your company alone, deleted on request.
